LEARN AS YOU GO

Reading movement words

These terms help describe how eyes may move across a line of text.

●

Fixation

A short pause when the eyes focus on one place, such as a word.

↶

Regression

An eye movement back to an earlier part of the text.

➜

Saccade

A quick movement of the eyes from one place to another.

↗

Saccade amplitude

The distance covered by the eyes during one saccade.

    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is my camera preview not appearing?

    Check that your browser supports camera access, that the page is served from localhost or HTTPS, and that permission has been allowed.

    Why is speech recognition unavailable?

    Speech recognition support differs by browser and device. Try a supported browser and check microphone permission. You can still review the other application sections.

    Does this application diagnose dyslexia?

    No. Neuro Vision is an experimental screening prototype. It is not a clinical diagnosis or a replacement for assessment by a qualified professional.

    Which measurements are currently available?

    The current frontend records participant details, reading duration and available speech transcript. Eye-movement analysis and model predictions require integration of the measurement pipeline and trained model.
